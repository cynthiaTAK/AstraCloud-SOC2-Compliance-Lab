# Phase 5 workpaper guide — simulated

1. Open `control_testing.csv` and review the 12 control objectives, ToD/OE procedures and linked fictional evidence IDs.
2. For each control, document the specific sample population, sample selection, dates, supporting artifacts, and reviewer sign-off **only after** actual evidence is available.
3. Use `findings_and_remediation.csv` to track the nine source alerts. Distinguish evidence requests from verified operating failures.
4. Record remediation owner, action, closure evidence and retest date; do not close findings without verification.
5. Read `executive_audit_report.md` for the management-facing summary and scope limitations.

**Training-only.** Not a CPA audit, SOC 2 opinion, certification, or proof of Vanta/Drata experience.
